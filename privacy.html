Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Hermes Shared Memory is a personal application designed to synchronize selected Google Drive files between AI tools authorized by the account owner.

Data Access

The application requests Google Drive access limited to files authorized for use with the app.

Data Use and Storage

Authorized files are used solely for the owner's personal synchronization workflow. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Data Sharing

Files may be processed by AI tools explicitly connected and authorized by the account owner.

Data Control

The owner can revoke Google account access through Google Account security settings.
